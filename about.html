ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

Who We Are

Rural Light Through Youth Platform (RLTY) is a youth-led rural community development platform that brings young people together to identify local challenges, discuss solutions, develop ideas and take practical action.

Our Vision

Rural communities where young people are inclusive, active, creative and confident contributors to development.

Our Mission

To create a practical youth-led platform connecting young people with community challenges, learning, creativity, innovation, leadership and action.
