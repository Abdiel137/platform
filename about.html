    ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

    Who We Are

    Rural Light Through Youth Platform (RLTY) is a youth-led rural community development platform that brings young people together to identify local challenges, discuss solutions, develop ideas and take practical action.

    Our Vision

    Rural communities where young people are inclusive, active, creative and confident contributors to development.

    Our Mission

    To create a practical youth-led platform connecting young people with community challenges, learning, creativity, innovation, leadership and action.

    WHAT GUIDES US

    Our Guiding Principles

    The Rural Light Through Youth Platform is guided by principles that promote responsible youth participation and positive community development.

    Unity

    Bringing young people and community members together to work towards shared goals.

    Patriotism

    Encouraging young people to love, respect and contribute positively to their country and communities.

    Youth Participation

    Ensuring young people have a meaningful role in decision-making and implementation.

    Inclusion

    Giving all young people fair opportunities to participate and contribute to their communities.

    Transparency

    Ensuring that roles, resources and results are communicated clearly.
