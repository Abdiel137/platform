How We Work

Our Working Approach

Rural Light Through Youth Platform follows a practical, participatory approach that enables young people to move from identifying local challenges to taking meaningful community action.

1. Identify

Young people identify challenges and opportunities within their communities.

2. Discuss

Participants share experiences, exchange ideas and understand the causes of identified challenges.

3. Create

Young people work together to develop possible solutions and practical ideas.

4. Test

Proposed ideas are explored and assessed to understand whether they can work in the local community.

5. Implement

Selected ideas are translated into practical community action.

6. Learn

Participants reflect on their experiences, evaluate progress and identify lessons.

7. Expand

Successful experiences are shared and opportunities to extend the approach to other communities are explored.
