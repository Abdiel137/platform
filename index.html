    WHO WE ARE

    Young People. Local Ideas. Real Change.

    Rural Light Through Youth (RLTY) is a youth-led rural community development platform bringing young people together to identify local challenges, discuss solutions, develop ideas and participate in practical implementation.

    OUR AREAS OF FOCUS

    Building Stronger Rural Communities

    Health

    Encouraging community action around health, hygiene, sanitation and drugs abuse challenges.

    Education

    Promoting youth participation in addressing educational challenges and opportunities.

    Agriculture

    Exploring locally relevant ideas around agricultural production and market opportunities.
