Working Together

Our Partners and Collaboration

Rural Light Through Youth Platform recognizes that meaningful community action requires cooperation, shared responsibility and strong relationships. We aim to gradually build partnerships with organizations and individuals who share our commitment to youth participation and community action.

Potential Partners

The platform seeks to develop collaborative relationships with:

Local Authorities

Supporting community coordination and local engagement.

Youth Organizations

Encouraging youth participation, leadership and collaboration.

Community-Based Organizations

Connecting local experiences with community initiatives.

Faith-Based Organizations

Building relationships with churches and other faith-based institutions.

NGOs and Development Organizations

Exploring opportunities for technical and program support.

Training Institutions and Individual Mentors

Providing opportunities for learning, guidance and mentorship.

Collaboration with Religious Churches

RLTY places particular importance on collaboration with religious churches. These relationships can support the coordination, communication and implementation of programs carried out through the platform.

Through this collaboration, churches may help connect young people with community activities, provide spaces for meetings and discussions, and support the implementation of agreed initiatives.

Areas of Partner Support

Depending on the needs of a particular program, partners may contribute through:

  • Meeting spaces and venues
  • Training and capacity support
  • Mentorship and guidance
  • Technical support
  • Materials and equipment
  • Networking and connections
  • Financial support

Partner With Us

We welcome organizations, churches, institutions and individuals interested in exploring collaboration with Rural Light Through Youth Platform.
