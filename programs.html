What We Discuss

Our Programs

Rural Light Through Youth Platform creates opportunities for young people to identify challenges, exchange ideas and develop practical community-led responses. Our discussions focus on three important areas of community life.

Health and Community Well-being

Encouraging young people to discuss health challenges and promote healthier, more responsible communities.

  • Alcohol and drug abuse
  • Malnutrition and food security
  • Hygiene and sanitation
  • Mental health and well-being
  • Disease prevention and health awareness
  • Community responsibility for health

Education

Creating space for young people to discuss educational challenges and the importance of learning in community life.

  • School dropout and absenteeism
  • Access to education
  • Family and community involvement
  • Study habits and personal discipline
  • Digital literacy and technology
  • Vocational education and practical skills

Agriculture

Bringing young people together to explore agricultural challenges and practical ideas that can support local farming communities.

  • Low agricultural production
  • Farming knowledge and practices
  • Soil fertility and land management
  • Water management
  • Crop and livestock diversification
  • Post-harvest losses and agricultural markets
